03 / Scholarly Infrastructure

Archives, Laboratories & Residential Commons

Designed to sustain focused intellectual work alongside vibrant collegiate camaraderie, every campus facility is accessible to all matriculated students.

1.8M+ Print Volumes · 24/7 Archival Access · Rare Manuscripts

The Sterling & Vance Research Library

Housing over 1.8 million bound volumes, rare first-edition scientific folios, and 640 acoustic study carrels with dedicated archivist consultation for undergraduate thesis writers.

Collegiate Athletics & Fieldhouse Complex

Championship hardwood courts, an eight-lane indoor track, rowers’ ergometer bays, and sports physiology studios supporting both varsity competitors and daily intramural recreation.

Seasonally curated menus sourced from regional organic farms, hosting daily communal meals and weekly evening High-Table dialogues between visiting laureates and students.
